Pros and cons of using AI

Potential benefits

  • Helps brainstorm, summarize, and get started on tasks.
  • Can explain concepts and support learning at your own pace.
  • Can save time on routine tasks and help organize ideas.
  • Offers language and accessibility support for different needs.

Potential drawbacks

  • May produce inaccurate, outdated, or biased information.
  • Sharing sensitive details may create privacy risks.
  • Over-reliance can limit independent thinking and skill-building.
  • AI output may not follow school, workplace, or copyright rules.

Benefits and risks depend on the tool and how you use it.

Use AI at your own risk.
